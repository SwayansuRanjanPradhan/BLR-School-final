Early Years (Nursery–UKG)

OUR CURRICULUM

Learning through exploration, play, stories, music, movement, and hands-on experiences.

Key Pillars of Growth

Core Focus Areas

Language Development

Developing listening, speaking, reading, and early writing skills through storytelling, phonics, vocabulary games, and expressive communication.

Numeracy Skills

Building foundational mathematical concepts including number sense, counting, patterns, shapes, spatial awareness, and logical thinking.

Social & Emotional Development

Fostering self-confidence, empathy, sharing, cooperation, emotion management, and building healthy peer relationships in a supportive environment.

Gross & Fine Motor Skills

Enhancing physical coordination, balance, agility, hand-eye control through outdoor play, gymnastics, sensory activities, and fine manipulation.

Creativity & Imagination

Encouraging artistic expression, imaginative role-play, music, dance, rhythm, drama, and open-ended creative exploration.

Environmental Awareness

Inspiring natural curiosity about nature, animals, weather, healthy living habits, and caring for the world around us.
